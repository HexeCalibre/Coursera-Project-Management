## Exemplar Assessment
Compare the exemplars to your completed activities (your meeting agenda and invitation email). Review your work using each of the criteria in the exemplars above. What did you do well? Where can you improve? Use your answers and the answers to the following questions to guide you as you continue to progress through the course. 

### Meeting agenda
- Does the agenda lists all core members of the team as “Attendees”? 

- Does the agenda include summary of the meeting’s purpose and expectations? 

- Does the agenda include three topics or next steps to discuss with your team? 

- Does each topic include how the issues will be approached?

- Do all approaches specify which team members will address the issue?

### Invitation email
- Does the subject line explain the purpose of the email? 

- Does the email include an opening that acknowledges the team’s work or something that is going well. 

- Does the email include a body section that explains the purpose of the meeting and proposes a meeting time?  

- Does the email include a signature (name) and the meeting agenda is listed as an attachment to the email?  

> **Note:** *The exemplars represent one possible way to complete the activities. Your work will likely differ in certain ways. What’s important is that your finished product is an example of a meeting agenda and invitation email.*
