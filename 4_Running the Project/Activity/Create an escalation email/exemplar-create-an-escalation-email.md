# Actovotu Exemplar: Create an escalation email

## Exemplar Assessment
After you complete your escalation email, you can compare your work to an exemplar. The reading assignment, 
[Writing an effective escalation email](/4_Running%20the%20Project/Module%201/writing-an-effective-escalation-email.md)
, includes a good example of an email for you to assess, along with an accompanying breakdown. As you review it, it may also help to check your email according to the following questions:   

- Does the email include the following elements: a subject line, greeting, body, and closing?

- Does the email maintain a friendly tone? 

- Does the email state your connection to the project? 

- Does the email explain the problem?  

- Does the email explain the consequences? 

- Does the email propose at least one action or solution? 

- Does the email make a request? 

> **Note:** *The exemplar represents one possible way to complete the activity. Your work will likely differ in certain ways. What’s important is that your finished product helps you learn the process of writing an escalation email.*
